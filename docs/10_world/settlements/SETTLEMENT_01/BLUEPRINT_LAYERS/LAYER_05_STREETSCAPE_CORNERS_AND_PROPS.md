# Layer 05 — Streetscape, Corners and Prop Sockets

Status: PROVISIONAL STREETSCAPE BLUEPRINT

## Rule

Corners must have a navigation or world-life function. Avoid decorative dead zones and random prop dumping.

Props use sockets so visual assets can be replaced without changing gameplay/layout ownership.

## S01 South Gate / Arrival

### South-west arrival/security corner
Planned sockets:
- guard notice board
- stacked supply crates
- wall/watch access
- lantern
- guard idle anchor

Keep a clear view from the gate to the central spine.

### South-east logistics corner
Planned sockets:
- water trough
- cart parking
- visitor/loadout crates
- lantern/sign
- loading interaction

### Inner south-west shoulder
- baggage/supply bay
- bench
- low fence or barrier only if it does not constrain arrival flow

### Inner south-east shoulder
- secondary cart socket
- wayfinding sign
- open standing/waiting area

## S02 Central Plaza

Fixed stall sockets:
- SW (-11,+8.5)
- SE (+11,+8.5)
- NW (-11,-8.5)
- NE (+11,-8.5)

Recommended identities:
- SW general goods/produce
- SE food/service
- NW local/civic rotating stall
- NE hunter/material/equipment display

Other sockets:
- public notice board near west/north plaza edge
- bench pairs outside central cross
- water/well/trough feature off the main X -4..+4 corridor
- temporary event socket in open plaza area
- 2–4 lightweight social idle sockets

Do not permanently fill the plaza center.

## S03 West Residential

Street-edge sockets:
- community hall sign
- two benches
- residence yard markers
- firewood pile
- small household storage
- limited garden/laundry-style visual props if consistent with art direction
- 2–3 lantern sockets

Rear/service sockets:
- waste/firewood/storage
- no heavy market clutter

## S04 East Work District

Street-edge sockets:
- smith sign

Rear/service sockets:
- ore pile

- tool-storage rack

## Wall modules

Planned reusable sockets/assets:
- straight wall segment
- inside corner
- outside corner
- gate connector
- watch-platform connector
- utility opening
- damaged/repair variant only where justified

Wall visuals must not be one giant scene/collider.

## Prop density rule

Primary navigation lanes:
- low clutter

Plaza perimeter:
- medium controlled clutter

Work rear/service zone:
- higher functional clutter

Residential:
- low-to-medium life props

Hunter exit:
- low clutter with strong readable preparation landmarks

## Collision rule

Small decorative props should default to no collision unless collision materially affects gameplay.

Large props:
- explicit simple collision
- no mesh-derived collision by default
