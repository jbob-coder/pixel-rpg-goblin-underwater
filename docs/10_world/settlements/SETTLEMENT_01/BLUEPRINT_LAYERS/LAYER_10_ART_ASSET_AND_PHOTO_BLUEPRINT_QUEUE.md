## Blueprint process

For each new visual:
1. source/provenance record
2. OBSERVED/SCALE-ANCHORED/INFERRED classification
3. orthographic/model sheet where useful
4. footprint/height target
5. modular breakdown
6. material group
7. collision class
8. interaction-anchor needs
9. runtime asset
10. automated integration
11. device visual check

## No-copy rule

References from other games/media may inform mechanics or broad visual categories only.

Do not reproduce:
- protected distinctive building designs
- logos
- named IP
- UI
- copyrighted textures/assets

Prefer original references, user-created/generated references, public-domain material, or clearly licensed sources.
