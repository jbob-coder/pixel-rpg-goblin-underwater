# Visual Reference Spec — Community Hall / Local Lodge

Status: READY FOR REFERENCE GENERATION  
Building blueprint:
`../BLD_01_COMMUNITY_HALL.md`

## Geometry constraints

Runtime-planning dimensions:
- footprint: 8×10 m
- wall height: about 3.8 m
- ridge: about 5.8–6.2 m
- primary facade: east
- real doorway: 1.8×2.4 m

The image must respect this compact scale.

## Visual identity

Target:
- sturdy frontier community lodge
- civic but not wealthy
- larger and more identifiable than residences
- simpler and less industrial than smith
- warm/local/social
- original IP

Avoid:
- palace
- church/cathedral
- tavern stereotype
- guild hall copied from known fantasy games
- giant fortress architecture
- overly ornate medieval decoration

## Silhouette

Preferred:
- simple rectangular body
- medium pitched roof
- slightly extended entrance canopy or beam emphasis
- clear central/local-hall identity
- one strong civic sign location

The silhouette should remain legible from the Central Plaza and frontage lane.

## Exterior material language

Primary:
- dark timber/wood framing
- stone lower base/foundation
- muted roof shingles
- restrained cloth/banner accent
- dark metal hardware

Reuse current Pixel RPG Pack 010 material families where practical.

## East facade

Must communicate:
- one obvious main entrance
- 1–2 windows
- hall sign/marker
- optional bench/lantern
- enough blank structural area for pixel readability

Do not cover the entrance in decorative props.

## Side/rear

North/south:
- simple windows
- visible timber structure
- low clutter

Rear:
- small storage/service identity
- no second grand entrance

## Roof

Preferred:
- simple pitched/gabled roof
- modular roof halves
- readable ridge
- no complex tower roof

Must support potential split/hide behavior if first-person interior handling needs it.

## Interior reference

Need a compact open hall.

Show:
- open central floor
- keeper desk/work zone
- notice/community wall
- 3–5 seating/gathering positions
- simple rear storage/meeting nook

Avoid:
- maze of rooms
- huge feast hall
- excessive furniture

## Modular breakdown

Desired modules:
- foundation/base
- wall panel A
- wall panel window
- wall panel door
- corner post
- roof left
- roof right
- ridge
- entrance canopy
- sign bracket
- window frame
- door frame

## Pixel/game readability

From first person:
- entrance visible from 10–20 m
- windows not too small
- timber/stone value contrast
- limited small trim
- sign silhouette more important than tiny text

## Generation prompt specification

Create an original compact frontier community hall for a first-person pixel-styled real-3D RPG. The building is approximately 8 m wide by 10 m deep, one story, around 3.8 m wall height, with a medium pitched roof reaching about 6 m. East-facing facade, one clearly readable 1.8 m wide doorway, simple timber framing over a stone base, muted shingles, restrained cloth accent, two readable windows, a small civic sign bracket, practical local construction, not ornate, not a tavern, not a church, not a castle. Show front, side, rear, top/roof, 3/4 perspective, and a simple interior cutaway with open central hall, keeper desk, notice wall, benches, and rear storage nook. Game asset reference sheet, neutral background, orthographic-style clarity, labeled modular parts, no watermark, no copied game design.

## Review checklist

Approve only if:
- size feels compact
- doorway readable
- hall visually distinct from residence/smith
- modular parts identifiable
- roof simple enough to build
- interior supports open circulation
- no copyrighted distinctive design
