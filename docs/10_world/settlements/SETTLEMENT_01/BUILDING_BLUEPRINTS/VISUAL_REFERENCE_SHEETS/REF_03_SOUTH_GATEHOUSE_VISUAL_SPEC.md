# Visual Reference Spec — South Gatehouse / Arrival Complex

Status: READY FOR REFERENCE GENERATION  
Building blueprint:
`../BLD_03_SOUTH_GATEHOUSE.md`

## Geometry constraints

South gate opening:
- 8 m clear

West gatehouse:
- 8×7 m
- one-story base with possible modest vertical watch identity

East watch:
- 6×6 m
- simpler companion structure

The gate opening must remain visibly open between them.

## Role

Civilian/visitor arrival threshold.

Visual message:
- protected settlement
- organized arrival
- approachable security
- local identity

This is not the Hunter Gate.

## Distinction from North Hunter Gate

South:
- more orderly
- carts/logistics
- visitor orientation
- civic banners/signage

North:
- more rugged
- warnings
- hunt prep
- watch/supply identity

## Composition

Preferred reference sheet should show the whole arrival composition:
- west gatehouse
- 8 m central opening
- east watch structure
- short wall connectors
- banner/sign sockets
- lantern positions

## West gatehouse visual

Target:
- compact administrative/guard building
- one readable interior entrance
- visible window/guard opening
- modest wall/watch connection

Avoid:
- fortress tower
- giant portcullis
- castle battlements

## East watch visual

Target:
- smaller support/watch building
- stronger vertical silhouette than a residence
- simple roof/watch cap
- upper platform may be visual-only in first pass

## Wall/gate language

Use modular:
- straight wall
- gate connector
- post/frame
- banner mount

Gate doors may be designed as future stateful visuals, but the normal reference should show the 8 m passage clearly readable.

## Materials

Settlement-consistent:
- timber beams/posts
- stone footings/base
- muted roof
- dark metal hardware
- limited cloth banners

## Arrival props

Visual reference may include:
- notice board
- crates
- water trough
- cart
- lantern
- wayfinding sign

Do not let props obscure the gate opening.

## Interior

West gatehouse:
- simple guard/admin room
- desk
- wall storage
- visitor conversation area

East watch:
- minimal guard room or covered position

## Modular breakdown

- west gatehouse body
- east watch body
- gate posts/frame
- gate doors
- straight wall modules
- left/right wall connectors
- banner mount
- lantern mount
- guard-window module
- roof modules

## Generation prompt specification

Create an original frontier settlement civilian arrival gate complex for a first-person pixel-styled real-3D RPG. The composition has a compact 8×7 m west gatehouse, a smaller 6×6 m east watch structure, and a clearly open 8 m central gate passage between them. Use sturdy timber construction, stone bases, muted roofs, dark metal hardware, restrained banners and lanterns. The gate should feel secure but welcoming, suitable for carts and visitors, not a fortress or castle. Show full front arrival elevation, rear/inside-settlement view, side views, top plan, 3/4 perspective, west gatehouse interior cutaway, and modular wall/gate connector parts. Neutral reference-sheet background, orthographic clarity, no copied game architecture, no watermark.

## Review checklist

- central gate opening obviously wide
- west/east masses do not visually pinch the path
- civilian arrival identity distinct from Hunter Gate
- modular wall/gate connectors understandable
- no fortress scale creep
- suitable for first-person readability
